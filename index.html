What's a heat pump?

Moving heat, not making it

Heat pumps are highly efficient electric HVAC systems that extract existing thermal energy from the outside air or ground and move it indoors or outdoors, depending on whether a home needs heating or cooling. Instead of burning fuel to generate heat, they simply relocate the heat that's already there — which is what makes them so efficient.

About this project

Why we're building this

This site supports a community-based research project focused on lowering the energy burden of residential rental properties in Hanover, NH.

Our focus

We're studying the obstacles renters face in benefiting from heat pump technology, and drafting a section of a regional grant application — the New England Heat Pump Accelerator (NEHPA) Community Grant — to address those obstacles directly.

We're working closely with the community partners authoring the grant, including the Town of Hanover and the Town of Lebanon, as well as the people the grant is meant to help: tenants and landlords.

How we're gathering information

Our research combines a survey of renters, landlords, and property managers with interviews of local officials, contractors, and past grant recipients, alongside data analysis of Hanover's rental housing stock and energy usage.

If you're a Hanover renter, landlord, or property manager and want to share your experience with heating and cooling systems, we'd love to hear from you — details coming soon.
